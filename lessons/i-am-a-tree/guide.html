Teacher guide: I Am a Tree 🌳

Crane and Dove · 30 minutes · Week 4 · No writing

What the children learn

A tree chant with actions. The children pick the one right word for each picture, say lines back after the voice, and use their whole body to show the words.

Before class

  1. Open the lesson once while the internet is on. After that it works even if the internet goes off.
  2. Turn the speakers up. The voice leads the lesson.
  3. Spread the children out so their arms can go wide.
  4. Press 📺 Full screen, then ▶ Let's grow! The first time, the lesson shows you the buttons.

What happens

  1. 4 min Hello. The voice says hello. Everyone stands, curls down small like a seed and grows tall.
  2. 16 min The chant, part by part. Tall or small, the wind, bees and birds, rain and sun, what a tree gives. In each part the children shout the answer to a picture question, say each line back after the voice, and do the moves. When the lesson stops and waits, the Next button glows: press it when everyone is ready.
  3. 5 min All together. The whole chant with no stops. Everyone says it with the voice and does the moves.
  4. 3 min Tree freeze game. Press Pause. Call a word: wind (sway), rain (wiggle fingers), bee (buzz), bird (flap), sun (stretch), seed (crouch small). The children move, then freeze like a tree.
  5. 2 min Goodbye. Big clap. Bye bye, trees!

The buttons

Next ▶When the lesson stops and waits, it glows. Press it when the children are ready. ⏸️ PauseStops the voice. Press Play to carry on. ◀ BackStarts this part again. Press it twice to go to the part before. ❓ HelpTells you what to do and say right now. 📺 Full screenMakes the lesson fill the screen. 🔊 SoundTurns the sound on or off. ⚪ ⚪ ⚪The circles at the top jump to any part.

If something goes wrong

  • No sound: check the speakers are on and turned up, and that the button says 🔊 Sound on.
  • The picture is small: press 📺 Full screen.
  • You lost your place: tap a circle at the top to jump to that part.
  • The children need a break: press ⏸️ Pause. Press Play when you are ready.

Teacher guide: My Promise 🤝

Crane and Dove · 30 minutes · No writing

What the children learn

A promise chant with actions, and how to give a chant a big finish. The big idea: a promise is when you say you will do something, and then you do it.

Before class

  1. Open the lesson once while the internet is on. After that it works even if the internet goes off.
  2. Turn the speakers up. The lesson has a voice that leads the children.
  3. Make space so the children can stand, clap and freeze.
  4. Press 📺 Full screen, then ▶ Start. The first time, the lesson shows you the buttons.

What happens

  1. 3 min Warm up. I PROMISE in a big voice, then a small voice. Shake, shake, shake, and freeze.
  2. 2 min Our earth chant. Say Big blue earth once together.
  3. 12 min Our promise chant. What is a promise? Then the voice says each line, the children say it back, and Next shows the action: roll the ball, pull a friend up, finger on lips, a big hug.
  4. 4 min The big finish. Clap, clap, and Next calls FREEZE. Practise it twice.
  5. 5 min Choose the ending. The children try two endings for the chorus, then clap for their favourite. Tap the winning card: the whole lesson uses that ending from then on.
  6. 4 min Goodbye. The chant all together with the big finish (record it for the class portfolio), then the whole poem on the last page.

The buttons

Next ▶The only thing that moves the lesson on. Nothing moves by itself, so take your time. 🔁 AgainSays the line again, as many times as you like. ◀ BackGoes back one page. 📖 PagesJump to any page. ⏭ Last page goes straight to the whole poem. ❓ HelpTells you what to do right now. 📺 Full screenMakes the lesson fill the screen. 🔊 SoundTurns all sound on or off.

If something goes wrong

  • No sound: check the speakers are on and turned up, and that the button says 🔊 Sound.
  • The picture is small: press 📺 Full screen.
  • You lost your place, or time is running out: press 📖 Pages and tap the page you want.

Pause and Try Again

Crane and Dove together · 30 minutes · No writing

Today’s idea: If our words come out too fast, we can pause, breathe and try again. If we miss a line, we can ask, “Again, please!”

Controls

Press N to show or hide the teacher notes on the lesson page. Hear this page plays the instruction. Again repeats the last sound on the page. Next and Back move only when pressed; Pages jumps to any part. Share tab audio in Google Meet. If the voice is unavailable, read the line and the teacher prompt aloud.

Lesson flow

3 min · Warm up: Breathe comfortably, say “Hello, friend!” Shake hands and freeze on “stop.”

2 min · Remember: Echo “Hand on my heart, I promise true.” Invite any ending the children remember.

5 min · Listen: Play the fast line. Ask what the children heard. Say “Again, please!” together, then play the clear line. Show an open hand for pause.

6 min · Try together: Echo each two-part example. Roll an imaginary ball. Older children can suggest another kind way to ask for a turn.

7 min · Build the poem: Read each line, leave time for an echo, and add the action.

I have something / I want to say. (point to self, then outward)
I pause a little / along the way. (open hand)
If you miss it / that is okay. (open both hands)
I take a breath / and try again. (breathe, gesture outward)

4 min · Play: Say “Hello, my friend!” Pause and freeze. Invite another short line.

3 min · Finish: Say the poem together. Record the class chant for the portfolio if practical. Ask what to say if we miss a line.

Short on time? Use one example instead of three, then keep the poem and closing performance.

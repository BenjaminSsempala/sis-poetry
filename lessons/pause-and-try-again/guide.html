Let’s Build a Poem

Crane and Dove together · 30 minutes · No writing

Today: Listen, reveal the words and say a six-line poem about a bird. The children can point, speak, echo and move.

Using the page

Press N to show or hide teacher notes. Back and Next move only when pressed. Again repeats the last sound. Pages jumps to a section. Share tab audio in Google Meet.

Lesson flow

4 min · Warm up: Breathe with the voice; it pauses after breathing in and out. Say hello. Shake hands and freeze.

3 min · Look and listen: Children point at the sun, tree and bird. Tap each card and echo the name.

14 min · Build: On each line, let the children call out. Tap tile 1, then tile 2. Play the full line and give everyone time to echo. Add actions: reach up, flap, cup an ear, sing, breathe and wave.

3 min · Who sings? Children point to the bird or tree; tap their choice. Encourage another try if needed.

3 min · Say the poem: Tap each line or play the whole poem; the voice leaves time between lines. Record the group if practical.

3 min · Move and close: Fly like a bird, freeze and share a favourite line. Finish with hello, clear and slow.

Our poem

The sun wakes up above the tree.
A little bird sings to me.
I stop and listen to its song.
I join the bird and sing along.
I take a breath and say hello.
My words come out clear and slow.

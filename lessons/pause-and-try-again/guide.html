Let’s Build Poems

Crane and Dove together · About 25–30 minutes · No writing

Today: Listen, build two poems, make weather sounds and let children choose, echo and move. Give them time for turns and repeats.

Using the page

Press N for teacher notes. Back and Next move only when pressed. Again repeats the last sound. Pages jumps to a section. Share tab audio in Google Meet.

Lesson flow

About 12 min · Bird and rain poems: Breathe with the voice, say hello, shake and freeze. Name the picture cards. Build and echo the six bird lines, then point to the pictures and reveal the four rain lines. Say both poems with actions.

About 3 min · Weather band: Listen to rain, wind and sunshine. Echo each sound and action; repeat in a new order. Invite different children to choose.

About 3 min · Weather order: Point to clouds, rain and sunshine in order. Tap Start again and give another child a turn.

About 4 min · New chant: Choose rain or wind. Reveal and echo each line with actions. Try the other sound; choose a favourite to say together.

About 3 min · Move and freeze: Let children choose bird, rain or sun. Move when the voice speaks and freeze when it says freeze. Different children can choose and lead. Finish with a favourite line and a clear hello.

Poems

The sun wakes up above the tree.
A little bird sings to me.
I stop and listen to its song.
I join the bird and sing along.
I take a breath and say hello.
My words come out clear and slow.

Grey clouds gather above the tree.
Raindrops fall in front of me.
I tap my hands like falling rain.
The sun peeks out and shines again.

Choose a chant

I hear the rain, drip, drip, drip.
I tap my hands, tap, tap, tap.

I hear the wind, whoosh, whoosh, whoosh.
I sway my arms, swish, swish, swish.

Let’s Build a Poem

Crane and Dove together · 30 minutes · No writing

Today: Listen, reveal the words and say poems about a bird and the rain. The children can point, speak, echo and move.

Using the page

Press N to show or hide teacher notes. Back and Next move only when pressed. Again repeats the last sound. Pages jumps to a section. Share tab audio in Google Meet.

Lesson flow

3 min · Warm up: Breathe with the voice; it pauses after breathing in and out. Say hello. Shake hands and freeze.

2 min · Look and listen: Children point at the sun, tree and bird. Tap each card and echo the name.

12 min · Build: On each line, let the children call out. Tap tile 1, then tile 2. Play the full line and give everyone time to echo. Add actions: reach up, flap, cup an ear, sing, breathe and wave.

1 min · Who sings? Children point to the bird or tree; tap their choice. Encourage another try if needed.

2 min · Say the bird poem: Tap each line or play the whole poem; the voice leaves time between lines. Record the group if practical.

6 min · Rain poem: Point to clouds and rain; tap the pictures to reveal and echo two lines. Wiggle fingers like rain. Tap hands, then tap the hands and sun pictures to reveal two more lines. Reach up for the sun. Say all four lines together.

4 min · Move and close: Fly like a bird, freeze and share a favourite line. Finish with hello, clear and slow.

Our poem

The sun wakes up above the tree.
A little bird sings to me.
I stop and listen to its song.
I join the bird and sing along.
I take a breath and say hello.
My words come out clear and slow.

Rain and sunshine

Grey clouds gather above the tree.
Raindrops fall in front of me.
I tap my hands like falling rain.
The sun peeks out and shines again.

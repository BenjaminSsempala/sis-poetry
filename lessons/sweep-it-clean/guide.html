Teacher guide: Sweep It Clean 🧹

Crane and Dove · 30 minutes · No writing

What the children learn

A cleaning chant with actions, and a big idea: we sweep away extra words so the shiny words are left. Not everything goes in the dustbin. Everything has a place.

Before class

  1. Open the lesson once while the internet is on. After that it works even if the internet goes off.
  2. Turn the speakers up. The lesson has a voice that leads the children.
  3. Make space so the children can stand and swing their arms.
  4. Press 📺 Full screen, then ▶ Start. The first time, the lesson shows you the buttons.

What happens

  1. 3 min Hello and warm up. Everyone stands, stretches up tall and gets ready to clean. Then shake off the dust together.
  2. 15 min Clean the classroom. Four jobs: the floor, the dustbin, the window, the books. For each job the children shout the answer to a picture question, say each line after the voice, then do the moves while you tap the mess on the screen to clean it. Then everyone says the chorus, louder each time.
  3. 6 min Mr Messy. He talks with too many words. The children copy his silly sentence, you press Next to sweep away the extra words, then everyone claps the words that are left. Then the children shout which words are dust (really, very, so, just) and you tap them.
  4. 3 min All together. Press Next and everyone says the whole chant with the moves.
  5. 3 min Cleaning freeze and goodbye. Press Call a job. The children do the move, then freeze like a broom. Press Say goodbye to finish.

The buttons

Next ▶Moves the lesson on. Press it when the children are ready. ◀ BackGoes back one step. 👆 TapTap the dust, papers, window and books to clean them. Or just press Next. ❓ HelpTells you what to do and say right now. 📺 Full screenMakes the lesson fill the screen. 🔊 SoundTurns all sound on or off. ⚪ ⚪ ⚪The circles at the top jump to any part.

If something goes wrong

  • No sound: check the speakers are on and turned up, and that the button says 🔊 Sound on.
  • The picture is small: press 📺 Full screen.
  • You lost your place: tap a circle at the top to jump to that part.
  • The children need more time: nothing moves on until you press Next, so take your time.

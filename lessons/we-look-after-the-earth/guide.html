Teacher guide: We Look After the Earth 🌍

Crane and Dove · 30 minutes · No writing

What the children learn

An earth chant with actions, the animals that share the earth with us, and clapping the beats in their names. The big idea: the earth looks after us, so we look after the earth.

Before class

  1. Open the lesson once while the internet is on. After that it works even if the internet goes off.
  2. Turn the speakers up. The lesson has a voice that leads the children.
  3. Make space so the children can stand, stamp and hop.
  4. Press 📺 Full screen, then ▶ Start. The first time, the lesson shows you the buttons.

What happens

  1. 3 min Warm up. HELLO EARTH in a big voice, then a small voice. Buzz like a bee, then sit small like a seed.
  2. 2 min Our cleaning chant. Say Sweep It Clean once together, with the brooms.
  3. 6 min Who lives here? A picture comes up and the children shout what it is. Press Next to show the name, then tap 👏 Clap for each beat: tree, bird, fish, frog (one clap), but·ter·fly, el·e·phant (three claps).
  4. 9 min The chant. The voice says a line, the children say it back, then Next shows the action. Use 🔁 Again as often as you like.
  5. 5 min Help the earth. The park is messy. The children call out each job and you tap: the bottles go in the bin, the tree grows, the bird comes home.
  6. 5 min Goodbye. The chorus once more, all together (record it for the class portfolio), then the whole poem on the last page.

The buttons

Next ▶The only thing that moves the lesson on. Nothing moves by itself, so take your time. 🔁 AgainSays the line again, as many times as you like. ◀ BackGoes back one page. 📖 PagesJump to any page. ⏭ Last page goes straight to the whole poem. 👆 TapTap the bottles, the tree and the bird in the park. Or just press Next. ❓ HelpTells you what to do right now. 📺 Full screenMakes the lesson fill the screen. 🔊 SoundTurns all sound on or off.

If something goes wrong

  • No sound: check the speakers are on and turned up, and that the button says 🔊 Sound.
  • The picture is small: press 📺 Full screen.
  • You lost your place, or time is running out: press 📖 Pages and tap the page you want.

The chant

ChorusBig blue earth,
so green and round,
we look after you,
all around!
Verse 1I water the tree,
drip, drop, drop.
Verse 2I fill the bird's cup,
sip, sip, sip.
Verse 3I pick up the bottle,
in the bin, plop!
Verse 4I walk round the frog,
hop, hop, hop.
